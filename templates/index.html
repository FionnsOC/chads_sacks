About the business

Chad's Sacks was funded in 1495 DR. We sell lether poches with protein powder in the heart of Waterdeep. Getting enough protein is vital for a balanced nutrition in these uncertain times. While other food businesses go under, we continue our mission to bring good quality protein powder to every citizen in Waterdeep. We are a very modern and diverse company, offering employment to people of various backgrounds and walks of life.
