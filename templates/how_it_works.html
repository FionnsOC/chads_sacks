Mechanics

For every day (in-game), and every vendor, roll 1d12. The result equals the number of bags sold. For the purposes of this, it's assumed that all bags cost 3 SP. A bag costs 5 CP to make. To calculate the profits, the earnings of each party member and of each employee (except Stefie), simply put the number of bags sold in the calculator.

Profit per bag: 3 SP - 5 CP = 25 CP

profit = bags × 25 CP
